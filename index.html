How it works

From gap list to report in five steps

The same sequence our diligence answers follow — so your vendor team can see exactly how member data moves before you ever send it.

Step 1

Agreements first

We sign a Business Associate Agreement and data-use terms with your plan before any member information changes hands.

Step 2

Secure list transfer

Your plan transfers the outreach list by secure file transfer — never by email — and approves the scripts and call cadence.

Step 3

Multilingual outreach

Agents call at the agreed cadence, in the member's language, honoring opt-outs and do-not-call requests on the spot.

Step 4

Scheduling & follow-up

We book with the provider's office, confirm with the member, remind before the visit, and reschedule when life gets in the way.

Step 5

Disposition reporting

You receive contacts, appointments, completions, and dispositions in the format and timeline your quality workflow needs.

Our boundary, in writing: we do outreach, reminders, and scheduling only. We do not sell or enroll members in plans, discuss plan benefits to persuade, or give medical advice — so our agents never need to cross into licensed or clinical work.

Security & compliance

How we handle member information

There is no government “HIPAA certification” — any vendor displaying one is showing you a self-assessment. What we can show you is the program itself:

BAA before any PHI

We sign a Business Associate Agreement before receiving any protected health information, and we work only to your plan's documented instructions for that data.

Trained agents, real safeguards

Agents complete HIPAA privacy and security training before taking calls and annually thereafter. We maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for the member information we handle.

Secure exchange only

Member lists move by secure file transfer under the BAA — never by ordinary email. Access is limited to the agents assigned to your campaign.

Calling rules, followed

Calls only between 8am and 9pm in the member's local time, with a real caller ID. Recording is announced on every call, and every “don't call me” is honored immediately.

Non-clinical, with escalation

Our agents are non-clinical. Clinical questions, urgent needs, and grievances are escalated to your plan or the member's provider under your protocol — never answered from a script.

U.S.-based team

Every role that touches member information is U.S.-based, in California. We do not use offshore staff for health plan member data.

Please do not send protected health information by email. Email is for campaign inquiries only. Member lists and any PHI are exchanged solely by secure file transfer, under a signed Business Associate Agreement.

FAQ

Questions vendor teams ask us

Do you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes — before we receive any protected health information. The BAA and data-use terms are step one of every engagement, ahead of any list transfer or script work.

How do we send you a member list?

By secure file transfer, under the signed BAA — never by email. Please do not send PHI to our email address. We work to your plan's format and confirm receipt and record counts before outreach begins.

Are your agents clinicians?

No. Our agents are non-clinical outreach staff. Clinical questions, urgent needs, and grievances or appeals are escalated to your plan or the member's provider under your protocol. We also do not sell or enroll members in plans, and we do not discuss benefits to persuade — outreach, reminders, and scheduling only.

What languages and hours do you cover?

Our own agents cover Vietnamese, Korean, and Chinese, with additional languages available on request. Campaigns run during the day, and our after-hours call center covers evenings and weekends for callbacks, overflow, and appointment confirmation.

What reporting do we receive?

Disposition and completion reporting in your format and on your schedule: attempts, contacts, appointments scheduled and kept, completions, and opt-outs — the inputs your quality team needs for its own measure workflow. A sample report is part of our vendor packet, available on request.

Can we start with a single measure or region?

Yes. Engagements can start with one measure, one region, or one member cohort, with the attempt cadence, reporting, and success criteria agreed in advance — and scale from there as your program needs grow.
